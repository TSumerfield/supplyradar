Who it is for

Built for a buyer who has to decide soon.

A good fit

  • You have an active requirement and a decision still open: a quote to benchmark, a supplier to replace, a first order to place.
  • The order is large enough to justify a check: roughly £3,000 or more for the first order, or £10,000+ of purchasing over the next 12 months.
  • You can share a specification, drawing, photo or an existing quotation.

Probably not a fit

  • General browsing or "what could I import?" with no specific product.
  • One-off orders too small for a £295 check to pay back. We will say so rather than take your money.
  • Needing someone to place orders, inspect production or arrange shipping. See "What it is not" below.

What it is not

A Supply Check is research and a recommendation. It does not include placing orders, holding money or goods, on-site factory inspection, production quality control or shipping. It does not guarantee any supplier's performance or a saving. If the evidence does not support a purchase, the report says so.

Method and evidence standard

Every figure says where it came from.

Supplier claims are never silently turned into facts. Each statement in the report carries one of these labels:

VERIFIED: checked against a first-party or official source, datedSUPPLIER-CLAIMED: stated by the supplier, not independently checkedESTIMATE: our calculation, assumptions shownUNKNOWN: not established

We work from your brief, supplier-provided quotations and documents, and public records. We do not invent suppliers, quotations or certifications, and we say UNKNOWN when we do not know.
