# SupplyRadar — Sample Procurement Decision Brief

> Anonymised demonstration of the reporting structure used for an initial Supply Check. Illustrative values are clearly marked; live client reports use supplier evidence and quotations.

## 1. Buyer requirement
**Product:** [Custom physical product]
**Quantity:** [e.g. 1,000 units]
**Destination:** United Kingdom
**Required delivery:** [date]
**Target / current cost:** [buyer-provided]
**Critical requirements:** specification, customisation, packaging, compliance, delivery window.

## 2. Demand / commercial case
Before supplier outreach, establish whether the requirement or product opportunity has evidence of durable demand. Separate:
- verified market evidence
- customer/review evidence
- estimates and assumptions
- reasons to reject the opportunity

## 4. Landed economics
Show factory price separately from:
1. tooling / setup
2. packaging
3. inspection / QC
4. freight
5. duty
6. VAT / taxes where applicable
7. other launch costs

**Estimated landed cost:** only after assumptions are explicit.

## 5. Verification
For each shortlisted supplier, record what has actually been checked. Examples:
- Chinese company registration / business licence
- manufacturer vs trading-company evidence
- factory address and operating history
- relevant certifications or test reports
- export history where available
- sample inspection
- video / factory visit where commercially justified

Supplier claims are never silently converted into verified facts.

## 6. Recommendation
**Proceed / investigate / reject**

Explain:
- why this supplier or product survives
- what evidence remains missing
- what must be checked before money is committed
- whether the economics justify the next step

## 7. Next decision
No purchase, sample payment or supplier commitment is made without buyer approval.
